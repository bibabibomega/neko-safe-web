FAQ

Are Neko Safe safes fire-resistant?

The mini and home models are mainly for theft protection and storing documents and small valuables. Fire resistance varies by model, so please ask the hotline about a specific model if you need it.

How do I open the safe if the battery dies?

Electronic safes usually have a backup key or emergency power contacts. Keep the spare key elsewhere and follow the manual for your model.

Can a mini safe be carried away?

A light safe can be carried off if not fixed down. Bolt it to the floor or cabinet, or choose a heavier model such as NS60 and above.
